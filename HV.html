Modulus Factory Automation

Overview

In the last year of my studies at Digital Arts and Entertainment I conducted my internship at Happy Volcano working on Modulus. I had the role of Technical and general Game artist. My responsibilities consisted of asset integration, vfx and shader development and game art creation, working closely together with the art lead. I also assumed and helped out with other miscellaneous game development tasks such as UI design and animation.

    Modulus

    Modulus is an automation game where you design your own systems and assembly lines to construct factories. The game is truly unique, the building blocks you design aren’t just resources, each one you craft becomes a visible part of a structure rising in real time. No two factories will look the same, your solutions are uniquely yours.

    Happy Volcano

    Happy Volcano is a Belgian indie game studio located in Leuven. Aside from Modulus, they are know for their previous titles such as; You Suck at Parking, The Alomst Gone and more.
